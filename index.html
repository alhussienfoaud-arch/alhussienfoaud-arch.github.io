About

From newsroom to social feed

I started as a video journalist at Al Masry Al Youm, filming and editing news reports and interviews on daily deadlines. Since 2020 I have run post-production for Obeikan Investment Group in Riyadh, editing content for digital and social channels and planning it with marketing and business teams.

My job is finding the strongest moment in a long piece of footage and building a short story around it that stays accurate.
